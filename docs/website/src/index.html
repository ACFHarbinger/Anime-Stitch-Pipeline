Constrained motion

Estimate lateral translation, vertical drift, and scale without allowing unstable homography warping to dominate anime cel pans.

GNC-TLS matching

Down-weight outliers along moving ink contours and preserve a stable global bundle adjustment under imperfect feature matches.

Seam-aware render

Route seams around protected character regions, blend overlaps, and retain diagnostics for visual and automated review.
